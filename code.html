Make a Paper Bridge

In this 45-minute practice workshop, participants will build and test a paper bridge to explore how folds help a structure support weight.

  • See the preparation checklist
  • View the numbered workshop agenda

Preparation checklist

Gather these materials before the workshop:

  • Ten sheets of ordinary printer paper
  • Two identical books to support each bridge
  • A handful of coins or other small, countable weights
  • A flat table and a pencil for recording results

Leave space between the books for the bridge to span.

Workshop agenda

  1. Welcome and question (5 minutes): Ask what makes a bridge sturdy.
  2. Plan (5 minutes): Sketch a paper bridge and predict how much weight it will hold.
  3. Build (10 minutes): Fold or shape one sheet of paper into a bridge between the books.
  4. Test and record (15 minutes): Add coins one at a time, count them, and note when the bridge bends or falls.
  5. Improve and compare (7 minutes): Change the folds, test again, and compare the results.
  6. Reflect (3 minutes): Share which shape held the most weight and why.
