Workshop overview

Purpose

Explore how folds and shapes affect how much weight a paper bridge can hold. You will make a prediction, test a design, improve it, and compare results.

Schedule

  1. Welcome and make a prediction (5 minutes)
  2. Plan and sketch a design (5 minutes)
  3. Build a bridge (10 minutes)
  4. Add weights and record results (15 minutes)
  5. Improve the design and reflect (10 minutes)

Equipment comparison

Bring the basic supplies; optional tools can help you compare designs.

Workshop equipment and its role
Item Use Needed?
Printer paper Forms the bridge; bring several sheets for redesigns. Required
Two matching books Support the paper bridge with a gap between them. Required
Coins or small weights Test and compare how much each design supports. Required
Ruler Measure the gap or bridge span. Optional
Pencil and paper Sketch designs and record test results. Recommended
